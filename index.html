Quantum Manipulations 2025

In a quantum system, observation alters reality — just as in media, framing determines perception. The collapse of a wave function into a definite state mirrors how propaganda and advertising shape public consciousness by reducing complex realities into singular narratives. This research investigates how immersion, nonconscious cognitive processes, and multisensory engagement contribute to ideological manipulation.

Blue is nice, red is better 2022

Throughout history, every mass medium, from written text to images and posters, from radio to television, whether interactive or not, has attempted to seduce us. Politics and commercial enterprises exploit these applications within our so-called attention economy. How does this form of manipulative mass communication influence public opinion and ultimately shape our idea of democracy?
