The focus of my artistic research is on how media and technology shape our political understanding of the world we live in. I am a media artist with both practical and theoretical training in Visual Arts, Cultural Studies, Information Science, Performance, and Sound Design. In 2000, I began experimenting with video and sound editing at the Higher Institute of Fine Arts (HISK), culminating in a residency at Cité des Arts in Paris. In 2008, I made the film Surface, which was selected for competition at FIDMarseille. After that, I created seven more essay films exploring the world of finance and markets, with a strong focus on media criticism. Supported by the VAF (Filmlab), these films were categorized as hybrid cinema and/or Expanded Cinema and screened at various film festivals and art institutions such as BOZAR, LE BAL, and WIELS. As a studio artist, my practice evolved from film and sound editing to 3D modeling, game engines, and coding languages. In my artistic research, I use techniques that shift and deconstruct our perception of reality by turning our human projections inside out. In my digital 3D worlds, I experiment - amongst other things - with lenses, moving an orthographic lens across a 3D surface. This process disrupts conventional ways of seeing, producing a speculative, rearranged world.
