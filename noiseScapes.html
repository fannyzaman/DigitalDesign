Noisescapes

Audiovisual Futurology

----------------------------------

Department of Speculative Facts

Noise landscapes or noisescapes. The texts accompani the images were partly created during ‘collective writing’ in the context of School of Darkness led by Sepake Angiama, in which I participated. It was part of the publication Speculative Facts editors Lietje Bauwens ad Quenton Miller published by Onomatopee. The workshop was organized by CIFAS and Kunstenfestivaldesarts in the context of the free school.
